Major characters from across the Resident Evil series.

01

GOVERNMENT AGENT

LEON S. KENNEDY

Rookie cop turned government operative.

FIRST APPEARANCE Resident Evil 2
AFFILIATION U.S. Government
KNOWN FOR Raccoon City Incident

Leon arrived in Raccoon City on his first day as a police officer and immediately became trapped inside the outbreak. Years later, he became a highly trained government agent involved in international bioterrorism cases.

S.T.A.R.S. MEMBER

JILL VALENTINE

Survivor of the mansion and Raccoon City disasters.

FIRST APPEARANCE Resident Evil
AFFILIATION S.T.A.R.S.
KNOWN FOR Nemesis Incident

Jill Valentine is one of the original S.T.A.R.S. members. She survived the Spencer Mansion incident and later fought to escape Raccoon City while being relentlessly pursued by Nemesis.

BIOHAZARD OPERATIVE

CHRIS REDFIELD

Soldier who spent decades fighting bioterrorism.

FIRST APPEARANCE Resident Evil
AFFILIATION S.T.A.R.S. / BSAA
KNOWN FOR Umbrella Investigation

Chris began investigating Umbrella after the Spencer Mansion incident. His career eventually evolved into a global fight against biological weapons and terrorist organizations.

SURVIVOR / ACTIVIST

CLAIRE REDFIELD

Determined survivor searching for her brother.

FIRST APPEARANCE Resident Evil 2
AFFILIATION TerraSave
KNOWN FOR Raccoon City Survivor

Claire entered Raccoon City looking for Chris Redfield and became one of the outbreak's survivors. She later devoted herself to helping victims of biological incidents around the world.

ADA WONG

An enigmatic spy whose loyalties remain complicated.

FIRST APPEARANCE Resident Evil 2
AFFILIATION Various Organizations

Ada operates in the shadows, frequently becoming involved in conflicts surrounding powerful biological weapons. Her missions repeatedly intersect with Leon Kennedy's.

06

UMBRELLA OPERATIVE

ALBERT WESKER

A central figure in the series' biological conspiracy.

FIRST APPEARANCE Resident Evil
AFFILIATION Umbrella / Various
KNOWN FOR Bioweapons Research

Wesker began as a S.T.A.R.S. captain while secretly working toward his own objectives. His involvement with Umbrella and experimental viruses made him one of the major antagonistic forces in the series.

SURVIVOR

ETHAN WINTERS

An ordinary man pulled into extraordinary horrors.

FIRST APPEARANCE Resident Evil 7
AFFILIATION Unaffiliated
KNOWN FOR Baker Incident

Ethan's search for his missing wife takes him to a remote Louisiana estate, where he becomes entangled in another biological nightmare. His story continues into the events of Resident Evil Village.

U.B.C.S. MERCENARY

CARLOS OLIVEIRA

Mercenary caught inside Raccoon City's final hours.

FIRST APPEARANCE Resident Evil 3
AFFILIATION U.B.C.S.
KNOWN FOR Raccoon City Evacuation

Carlos is a member of the Umbrella Biohazard Countermeasure Service who encounters Jill during the Raccoon City outbreak. Despite working for Umbrella's mercenary force, he helps survivors escape the collapsing city.
